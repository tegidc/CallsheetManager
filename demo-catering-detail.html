Catering detail — four ways

The same three people and four days in each. Everything is clickable. The header's B L D letters turn that meal on or off for everyone beneath, as catered — that is unchanged in every option; what differs is how a person's own days are opened and how a meal is made a per diem.

02

Two ticks per meal — Catered and Per diem

▸ opens one line per day. Each meal has two small ticks under CAT and PD; ticking one clears the other. Plain checkboxes, no cycling to learn, but nine ticks a day is a lot of boxes.

03

No ▸ at all — three small cycling marks in every day cell

The day cell IS the detail: three small boxes (B L D) that cycle — / ✓ / £, so everything is on one line and nothing opens. Densest, but it is the "three ticks a day" look you asked to lose, with a third state on top.

04

One tick per meal, a "PD" tag to flip it

▸ opens one line per day: a normal checkbox per meal (catered), and beside each a small PD tag — click it and that meal becomes a per diem (the tag lights amber, the box empties). The tick stays a tick; the exception is a tag.
